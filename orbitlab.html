How this works

  • TLE — a two-line element set, the 69-character format NORAD has published since the 1960s.
  • SGP4 — the simplified perturbations model that turns those elements into a position, accounting for drag and the Earth's equatorial bulge.
  • Accuracy is roughly a kilometre near the element epoch, degrading over days. That is why the app re-fetches.
  • Colour is the orbital regime: green LEO, gold MEO, pink the geostationary belt, violet high or elliptical.
  • If CelesTrak cannot be reached, a bundled snapshot flies instead and the status line says so.
