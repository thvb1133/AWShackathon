What you are actually looking at

  • Positions — heliocentric coordinates from J2000 elements, propagated with Newton–Raphson on Kepler's equation. Accurate to a fraction of a degree over centuries.
  • Halley's Comet flies its true 76-year ellipse. Push the clock to 2061 and watch it come back.
  • Voyager 1 and 2, Pioneer 10 and New Horizons recede along their real ecliptic bearings at their published rates.
  • Distances are compressed by default, or the inner planets would be invisible specks. Switch to true scale to feel how empty the Solar System really is.
  • Surfaces are generated in code at page load — no image files are downloaded at all.
